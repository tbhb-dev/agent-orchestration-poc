The repository preserves two earlier research collections under `research/imported/`. `agent-peering-tests/` studies peer identity, messaging, and sandbox behavior. `agent-session-tests/` studies interactive sessions and harness control.

[The import manifest](https://github.com/tbhb-dev/agent-orchestration-poc/blob/main/research/imported/MANIFEST.md) records the September 26 copy, source checksums, exclusions, and secret scan. It includes 117 copied files. Imported bytes remain unchanged, and CI rejects modifications or deletions. Synthesis belongs in new documents.

Phase 3 [issue #34](https://github.com/tbhb-dev/agent-orchestration-poc/issues/34) produces this project's research synthesis. It will distinguish established findings, container-related changes, open questions, and version-sensitive findings needing another test. Research gates under `research/gates/` already support the [conventions](/guides/).
