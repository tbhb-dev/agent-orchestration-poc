Design pages arrive in phase 4 after research and experiments establish the runtime behavior. The [design sketch](https://github.com/tbhb/agent-orchestration-poc/tree/main/design-sketch) is the starting point until then.

The sketch can change as evidence arrives. Operator requirements remain binding unless the operator approves a change. Phase 4 converts the supported design into site pages and [decision records](/decisions/), then retires the sketch in a commit pointing to its replacements. See the [phase 4 plan](/project/plan/#phase-4-design-in-the-docs-site).

- [GitHub event monitor](/design/github-event-monitor/), [untested] proposed for issue #98 before development. Covers webhook intake, local state, relay waits, and the immediate REST stopgap.
